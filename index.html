About Our Product

FreshCrunch is a healthy and fresh pickle product made from selected ingredients such as cucumber, carrot, and radish. We process these ingredients with natural fermentation to maintain the taste, crunchiness, and nutrition. Our products are perfect to be enjoyed as a side dish or healthy snack.

Product Description

Cucumber Pickles: FreshCrunch pickled cucumbers have a crunchy texture and a refreshing sweet-sour taste, suitable for all kinds of dishes.

Carrot Pickles: Our pickle carrots are rich in flavor and retain the natural crunch of carrots, providing a fresh sensation in every bite.

Radish Pickles: FreshCrunch pickled radishes offer a slightly spicy natural flavor with a distinctive aroma, making the eating experience more colorful.
